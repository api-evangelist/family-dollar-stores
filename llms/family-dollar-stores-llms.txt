# Family Dollar Stores

> Family Dollar is a US discount variety store chain selling consumables, household goods,
> apparel, seasonal merchandise and electronics in the $1–$10 price range, with stores
> concentrated in neighborhood and food-desert locations. Dollar Tree, Inc. divested the
> Family Dollar business on 7 July 2025 to Brigade Capital Management and Macellum Capital
> Management; Family Dollar now operates as a standalone private retailer headquartered in
> Chesapeake, Virginia.

## API surface

> Family Dollar publishes NO public API, developer portal, SDK, webhook catalog, MCP server
> or agent card. This was measured on 2026-09-07, not assumed: `/openapi.json`,
> `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/llms.txt` and the full RFC 8615 `/.well-known/` path list
> were probed on www.familydollar.com, familydollar.com, corporate.familydollar.com and
> careers.familydollar.com, and every one returned 404 (or a redirect out of the namespace).
> The subdomains `api.familydollar.com` and `developer.familydollar.com` do not resolve.
> The full static sitemaps of both the consumer site and the corporate site were read; no
> developer-facing page exists on either.
>
> The storefront at www.familydollar.com runs on Oracle CX Commerce and its tenant REST
> layer (`/ccstore/v1/`) answers anonymously, but that is Oracle's product contract running
> under Family Dollar's hostname — it is undocumented by Family Dollar, carries no published
> specification, terms of use, or support commitment, and is NOT catalogued here as a
> Family Dollar API. Supplier integration runs through the SAP Ariba Network vendor portal,
> a host platform Family Dollar buys rather than an interface it publishes.
